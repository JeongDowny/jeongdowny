* '명함소개팅' :cupid::Card Blind Date is a web service that allows users with a verified university email to exchange digital cards containing brief personal information, helping them meet new people. Users can create their own card by entering details such as age, admission year, MBTI, hobbies, and contact information. They then receive a card from the opposite gender, either randomly or based on recommendations. The platform is designed to maintain anonymity while facilitating natural conversations with minimal information. 
 I worked on the backend, handling user authentication via external APIs, designing and implementing the recommendation system, and connecting with open AI. I also contributed to testing and optimization, and was involved in the deployment and ongoing maintenance of the project. You can find the repository here: [Github Repository](https://github.com/digitalCard-date-service/work_space)
